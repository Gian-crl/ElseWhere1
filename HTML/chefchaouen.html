📍 Chefchaouen, Morocco

A PLACE TO REMEMBER

About the Place

Chefchaouen is a mountain town in northern Morocco known for blue-painted streets, traditional architecture, and a relaxed medina. Its winding alleys, local craft shops, and nearby natural scenery offer a memorable cultural escape.

Before You Go

A little preparation makes the journey smoother.

  • Wear comfortable shoes for steep, uneven streets.
  • Ask before photographing residents or entering private spaces.
  • Carry some local currency for small purchases and cafés.
  • Dress respectfully and check transport times for day trips.
