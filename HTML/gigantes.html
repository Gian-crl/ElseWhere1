Isla Gigantes

📍 Carles, Iloilo, Philippines

A PLACE TO REMEMBER

About the Place

Isla Gigantes is an island group in Carles, Iloilo, known for its beaches, rock formations, sandbars, and island-hopping trips. It is a great choice for travelers who enjoy coastal scenery and time on the water.

TRAVEL SMART

Before You Go

A little preparation makes the journey smoother.

  • Book a responsible boat operator and confirm the itinerary and inclusions.
  • Bring drinking water, sun protection, and a dry bag for valuables.
  • Follow safety advice around waves, tides, and rocky shorelines.
  • Avoid leaving litter and never remove shells or other natural items.
