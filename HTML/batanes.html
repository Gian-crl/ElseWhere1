Batanes

📍 Batanes, Philippines

A PLACE TO REMEMBER

About the Place

Batanes is celebrated for its rolling green hills, rugged coastlines, traditional Ivatan stone houses, and peaceful rural scenery. Its landscapes and local culture make it a distinctive destination in the northern Philippines.

TRAVEL SMART

Before You Go

A little preparation makes the journey smoother.

  • Check flight schedules and weather conditions before confirming plans.
  • Bring sun protection and a light jacket for windy viewpoints.
  • Ask permission before photographing residents or private property.
  • Follow local guidance and avoid standing close to cliff edges.
