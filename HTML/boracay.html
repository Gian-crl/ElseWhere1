Boracay

📍 Malay, Aklan, Philippines

A PLACE TO REMEMBER

About the Place

Boracay is best known for White Beach, soft sand, clear water, watersports, and a wide choice of places to eat and stay. Visitors can balance relaxed beach time with activities and evening entertainment.

TRAVEL SMART

Before You Go

A little preparation makes the journey smoother.

  • Check beach rules and current environmental guidelines before your visit.
  • Compare accommodation and activity costs, especially during busy periods.
  • Use sun protection and drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Choose authorized operators for boat trips and water activities.
