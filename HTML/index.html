About Us

We are a group of students passionate about travel,
exploration, and sharing beautiful places with you.

Our Purpose

ElseWhere is a travelogue website created to help people discover beautiful destinations in the Philippines and around the world. We provide useful information about tourist attractions, activities, travel tips, and other helpful details for travelers.

Our goal is to make discovering new places easier and more enjoyable by providing clear and accessible travel information in one website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about ElseWhere.

What is ElseWhere?

ElseWhere is a travelogue website that features different local and international destinations, travel information, activities, and helpful tips.

What destinations are featured on the website?

The website features selected destinations from the Philippines and different parts of the world.

Are the destinations local or international?

Both. ElseWhere features destinations from the Philippines as well as international destinations.

Where can I find travel tips?

You can find useful travel information and tips on our Travel Guide page.

How can I contact the team?

You can contact us through the contact form and the social media information provided below.
