Palawan

📍 MIMAROPA, Philippines

A PLACE TO REMEMBER

About the Place

Palawan is known for striking limestone formations, clear coastal waters, lagoons, reefs, and island-hopping routes. Popular areas offer opportunities for kayaking, snorkeling, and exploring coastal landscapes.

TRAVEL SMART

Before You Go

A little preparation makes the journey smoother.

  • Reserve popular boat tours in advance and confirm safety equipment.
  • Use reef-safe practices and avoid touching coral or marine wildlife.
  • Bring sun protection, a reusable water bottle, and a waterproof bag.
  • Check local weather and sea conditions before boat trips.
